What I do

Delivery and the client relationship are the core, and they travel together. Tap either to open it.

Programme delivery and client ownershipmain focus

Scoping, estimation and staffing — and the account, commercial side included, alongside it.

On the delivery side: scoping engagements, producing and owning effort estimates, staffing them against real capacity, tracking deliverables against signed agreements, forecasting and quarterly planning, roadmap intelligence, executive and phase reports, and delivery syncs.

On the client side: recurring calls with agendas and follow-through, stakeholder rhythms tiered by seniority, request triage, escalations and post-incident conversations; retainer health read as a signal rather than a number, shaping the service offering around what the client actually needs, proposals and work orders, renewals, and quarterly client reports.

The two halves travel together — it is one job. Reconciling what gets invoiced with what was actually delivered only works when the person tracking the deliverables is also the person the client talks to. Including the part most people avoid: pushing back, with technical justification, when a request conflicts with doing the work properly.
